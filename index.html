Before you apply

A few honest questions.

Can't I just be tested by a kinesiologist?

Yes. A kinesiologist can test on your behalf. The difference is that Heal is designed to teach you how to self-test, so the method does not remain entirely in the practitioner's hands.

Is the app just another thing I will stop using?

The app is not the product. It guides the 60-day sequence, shows you what to work through next and keeps the process clear between your calls and sessions. The skill itself is the product.

Why would this help when other things haven't?

Heal does not promise a breakthrough or guarantee an outcome. It gives you a different way to explore the pattern: rather than analysing the story again, you learn to test what may be sitting underneath it.

Is Heal therapy?

No. Heal is a personal-growth program for exploring beliefs and emotional patterns. It is not therapy, medical treatment or diagnosis, and it is not a substitute for professional care.
